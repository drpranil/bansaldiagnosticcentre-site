Privacy policy

Last updated: 23 November 2026

This policy explains how Bansal Diagnostic Centre, 11/1 North Rajmohalla, Indore, Madhya Pradesh 452002 ("we"), handles the personal information of patients and visitors.

What we collect

  • Details you give at registration: name, age, sex, mobile number, address and referring doctor.
  • Your test requests, results and reports, and the forms the law requires for certain tests.
  • Payment records for the tests you take.
  • Messages you send us by phone or WhatsApp.

This website itself does not use cookies, does not run advertising or tracking, and does not collect any information from you.

How we use it

  • To carry out your tests and prepare your reports.
  • To send you your reports, appointment confirmations and payment receipts, including by WhatsApp to the mobile number you gave us.
  • To share reports with the doctor who referred you.
  • To keep the records that Indian law requires, including under the PC&PNDT Act.

Who we share it with

We do not sell your information. We share it only with your referring doctor, with an outside laboratory when a test has to be sent there, with service providers that deliver our messages (such as WhatsApp), and with authorities when the law requires it.

How long we keep it

We keep medical records for as long as the law and good medical practice require.

Your choices

You can ask us for a copy of your reports, ask us to correct your details, or ask us to stop sending you WhatsApp messages. Reply "STOP" on WhatsApp or tell us at the reception.
